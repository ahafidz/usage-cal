⚖️ How to track progress

  • Take 7-day scale averages, ignore daily fluctuations.
  • Measure waist once per week in the same spot.
  • If weight/waist stalls for 3 consecutive weeks with good adherence, drop 100-150 kcal.
  • Take monthly photos. 2kg of muscle gain masked by 2kg of fat loss will look radically different despite the scale not moving.
