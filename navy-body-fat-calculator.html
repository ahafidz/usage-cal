The Male U.S. Navy Circumference Formula
1

Metric Formulation (Hodgdon & Beckett)

BF% = 495 / (1.0324 - 0.19077·log₁₀(Waist - Neck) + 0.15456·log₁₀(Height)) - 450

2

Why Neck Circumference Matters

The neck serves as a skeletal and lean tissue surrogate. An increase in neck thickness with identical waist circumference signals higher muscularity and bone structure, decreasing the calculated body fat percentage.

3

Waist-to-Neck Ratio Insight

Abdominal adipose stores track strongly with the waist-to-neck differential. Reducing waist circumference through caloric restriction while preserving neck mass through neck and trap hypertrophy optimizes the Navy body composition score.
